### About

I build travel on the ground and software off it.

- 🏔 Started travelling at thirteen. Night trains from Moradabad to Ramnagar.
- 🧭 Years of trip leading since — Spiti Valley, Udaipur, then Vietnam.
- 🇻🇳 Twenty-eight batches through Vietnam. Ho Chi Minh City → Da Nang → Hoi An → Hanoi → Ninh Binh → Ha Long.
- 🛠 Engineering background: a hardware project, defence side, can't say much.
- 🚀 Now building **Vamos Journeys** — a Vietnam-first DMC running white-labelled ground operations for travel companies.
- 📫 Reach me: **shauryaleo7@gmail.com**

### What I'm working on

- Vamos Journeys — vendor network, costing systems, and the tooling that keeps a trip from breaking at 6am.
- Internal tools for travel ops: costing models, rate cards, pipeline trackers. Sheets first, code where it earns its place.
- Learning: AI/ML, and how to apply it to operations rather than demos.
